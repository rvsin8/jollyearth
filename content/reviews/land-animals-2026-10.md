Verification used indexed IUCN assessment/DOI text, IUCN taxonomic search results, IUCN SSC Canid Specialist Group, IUCN recovery announcements, the assessor BirdLife's Red List factsheets, and the original tamarin assessment PDF. The direct Red List website returned HTTP 403 in this environment; no browser API or token was used. Assessment and DOI links are retained per record. Review dates are not represented as assessment dates.

No population counts are published for these ten records. Historical assessment estimates and newer programme censuses use different dates and units; neither is presented as a current estimate.

Editorial distinctions:

- Mountain gorilla's EN category is for the subspecies, not all eastern gorillas.
- Iberian lynx is VU, retaining its recovery story without obsolete CR/EN labels.
- Golden lion tamarin's dated IUCN trend is decreasing; subsequent programme recovery reports are described separately.
- Javan rhinoceros's stable trend is explicitly dated to its 2019 assessment. The archival zoo photograph is labelled historical.
- Saola have wild camera-trap photographs; the unsupported claim that no scientist has photographed one alive in the wild is not reproduced.
- Hirola is a grassland species. Its requested coastal hotspot association is a regional teaching connection, not a claim that it inhabits closed forest.
- No unsupported "rarest canid" superlative or estimated population is included.

Seven new SVG portraits join three existing portraits, with no duplicate species. No GLB assets are fabricated. Photo fallback assets are locally compressed WebP files with source and license records; navigation always uses the SVG icons. The build compiles reviewed profiles from the manifest and a legacy GBIF refresh reapplies these records instead of overwriting their verified categories.
