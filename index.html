● Grassland

Coastal Plain

Southeastern North America
GrasslandBiodiversity hotspotHabitat loss

Longleaf pine savannas, wetlands and coastal landscapes hold a rich native flora. Fire and water sustain the region’s distinctive ecological patterns.

Development and the loss of natural fire regimes reshape these habitats. Conservation includes restoring processes alongside protecting land.

Field notes

Turning point
The forest that needs to burn

Longleaf pine systems are shaped by frequent low-intensity fire. Restoration requires recovering ecological process, not only acreage.

Happening now
Connecting isolated habitat

Corridors and prescribed fire are increasingly used together so species can move while ecosystems retain their historic structure.

Setback
Protection alone is not enough

Climate pressure and altered disturbance regimes can undermine gains even inside formally protected places.
